# Muzzle flash sprite

Built-in image-generation tool; original PNG and alpha preserved. Four-frame 2×2 atlas, selected in Godot. Installed as `assets/weapons/test_rifle/muzzle_flash_v1.png` in the game repository. Existing hip/ADS gun sprites are relit by `scripts/weapons/weapon_flash.gdshader` during each flash, preserving their exact alignment and alpha.

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: transparent muzzle-flash animation sprite atlas for a gritty illustrated first-person WW2 wood/steel rifle. ONE square PNG, 1024x1024, strict invisible 2-column x 2-row grid of four 512x512 cells. Read top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right. Every cell contains exactly one centered muzzle-flash burst with its ignition origin at the exact center of its cell (256,256), no gun or hands. Four successive frames: small white-hot ignition with short amber spikes, full bright irregular star-shaped white-yellow flash with branching orange flame tongues, contracting orange-yellow burst, small fading amber wisps/sparks. Natural asymmetric combustion, restrained short rifle blast, not a grenade/fireball and not a cartoon explosion. Flat hand-painted illustrated VFX, warm ivory core, gold/amber/orange edges, crisp silhouette with soft luminous flame edges. Aiming toward the viewer's direction means mostly radial starburst shape, slight diagonal variation okay. Each burst stays within its cell with at least 40px transparent margins; largest burst about360px diameter. True alpha transparency outside flames, including gaps between flame tongues. No background, black matte, colored rectangle, checkerboard artwork, smoke cloud, text, numbers, gridlines, crosshair, gun, scene or borders. Keep each ignition center fixed so atlas animation doesn't jump.
