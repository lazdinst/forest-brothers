# Training prototype art — generation provenance

Mode: built-in image generation. Source PNGs are preserved unchanged. These are prototype candidates, not final approved animation or weapon specifications. Walking atlases use a 2×2 four-frame layout, sampled with Godot AtlasTexture; opposite profile is mirrored. First-person action motion is procedural over one generated hands/rifle image, not a complete authored hand-animation set.

## Walking front

Use case: identity-preserve. Create ONE transparent walking-animation sprite atlas for the approved fictional older civilian from Forest Brothers. The reference image specifies the same character, outfit, proportions, muted palette and gritty hand-painted illustration style. Preserve cap, grey hair/moustache where visible, brown patched jacket, grey sweater, dark trousers and worn boots. Empty hands. Do not redesign or add equipment.
Layout is STRICT: one portrait PNG, 1024 pixels wide by 1536 pixels high, divided invisibly into a 2-column by 2-row grid. FOUR separate full-body frames, each occupying its own 512x768 cell. Read order: top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right. Every frame shows the ENTIRE character head-to-boots, centered horizontally in its own cell, same figure height (~720 pixels), same ground baseline 738 pixels from the top of each cell. All feet remain inside their own cell. No overlap between cells. Invisible cell boundaries; no outlines, text, numbers, labels or floor.
Animation: a restrained four-frame walking cycle. Frame 1 contact pose with left leg forward/right leg back and opposite arms; frame 2 passing pose; frame 3 opposite contact pose with right leg forward/left leg back and opposite arms; frame 4 opposite passing pose. Natural elderly walking, modest stride, stable torso/head height, coherent anatomy. Arms/legs visibly change between frames; these are not four identical idle poses. Consistent eye-level orthographic camera, proportions, light and palette. Broad painted shading, clear silhouette, no photorealism or cartoon exaggeration.
True transparent alpha outside each figure, no scenery, haze, cast shadow, checkerboard artwork, text, weapon, additional character, or inset. Four frames of the SAME man.
All four frames: FRONT VIEW, walking toward the camera without changing orientation. Whole body remains frontal; preserve jacket front, sweater, face and front trouser repairs.

## First-person hands/rifle

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: one first-person hands-and-rifle foreground sprite for the gritty painted first-person game Forest Brothers, Latvia around 1946. Create a single 1024x1024 transparent PNG. First-person view from the player's eyes: two anatomically coherent bare adult hands in worn brown/olive civilian wool coat sleeves holding a worn wood-and-dark-steel Soviet SVT-40-inspired self-loading rifle with iron sights and a modest detachable box magazine. Right hand at the grip, left hand supporting the fore-end. The weapon extends from the lower right quadrant toward the upper middle, muzzle pointing into the distance near the horizontal center. Correct strong first-person foreshortening: rear receiver/butt larger near viewer, barrel smaller into distance. Both forearms enter naturally from the bottom edges; no visible face, torso or legs. Keep the muzzle and all weapon parts within canvas with clear margin; the arms can exit the bottom edge. Gritty hand-painted illustrated broad shading, muted brown/olive/charcoal colors, clear forms rather than photo microtexture, compatible with a low-resolution game. Mild neutral lighting, no muzzle flash, no smoke, no dramatic shadows, no ground. True transparent alpha outside the hands and rifle, absolutely no scenery, halo, vignette, frame, text, logo, UI, crosshair or watermark. No modern tactical accessories, optics, rail mounts, gloves, plastic stock or oversized magazine. This is a prototype visual, not a technical schematic.

Historical reference for the test rifle's period and ten-round detachable magazine: [Australian War Memorial collection](https://www.awm.gov.au/collection/C246039). The generated picture is an illustrative prototype, not a verified technical reconstruction or a locked final weapon roster.
